# Stone Family Recipe Book — Original Manuscript Accuracy Audit

**Status:** Publication corrections made; **NOT a certification that every ingredient and direction has been individually verified**.

**Current published set:** 244 distinct recipe entries from the 199-page original manuscript, with source-page links.

### Corrections already established from source images

- Original page 146 is **one recipe, Marble Squares**, not independent Chocolate Sour Cream Cake and Marble Square entries. The complete chocolate batter and cream-cheese swirl are reunited; original quantities corrected.
- Original pages 113–114 contain one Apple Upside Down Cake; the duplicate Apple Skillet Cake was consolidated.
- Original pages 195–196 contain one Beef Pot Roast recipe and photo continuation; the duplicate Pot Roast entry was consolidated.
- Page 191's Tangy Mushroom Sauce had been incorrectly titled Filet Mignon Sauce, with incorrect butter and wine quantities.
- Original page 13's Chive-Cucumber Sauce for Fish had a wrong ingredient list and was corrected, as was its Chive Butter paragraph.
- Original page 101 is Sausage Casserole Soufflé, not 'Sausage Besserol Seley'.
- Original page 138 contains Brownies, not 'Somes (4-H #.)'.
- Original quantity corrections from source scans: Coffee Cake flour; Fresh Carrot Pudding carrots; Eggplant Casserole Parmesan; Spinach Casserole sour cream.
- Eleven omitted original recipes added from pages 39 (10 fish sauces) and 154 (Oven Pancake with Applesauce); page 39 Tomato Sauce remains explicitly incomplete because its printed clipping cuts off.
- Overnight/multi-day timeline data now retains step starts beyond 24 hours. The original cooking blueprint design is unchanged.

### Verification limits

- This checklist records whether a **complete, line-by-line visual manuscript comparison** has been completed. All checkboxes remain unchecked until that has actually occurred. A corrected title or ingredient alone does not imply full verification.
- DeepSeek-engineered schedules have many estimated times. Estimated cooking times are not facts printed in the manuscript.
- **10 recipes** still have complete AI-reconstructed directions because no original directions were recovered; **33** contain selective AI source-text repairs. These must remain labeled and be checked against original scans where legible.
- Automatic OCR and text-pattern comparison were used to prioritize suspicious records; OCR is not reliable enough to certify quantities or all boundaries by itself.
- Distinct recipes may share an original page or title; a duplicate name alone is not proof of an erroneous duplicate.
